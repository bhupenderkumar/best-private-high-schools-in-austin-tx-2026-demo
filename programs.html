Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-K program is designed for young learners who are eager to explore and discover the world around them. Our experienced teachers provide a nurturing environment that encourages social, emotional, and cognitive growth.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program is built on a solid foundation of academic excellence, creativity, and character development. Our students learn through hands-on activities, projects, and experiments that make learning fun and engaging.

👨‍🎓

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School programs are designed to challenge and inspire students as they transition to more advanced academic and personal responsibilities. Our experienced teachers provide guidance and support to help students navigate these critical years.

🔬

STEM

Our STEM program is designed to foster a love of science, technology, engineering, and math in students. Our experienced teachers provide hands-on training and mentorship to help students develop the skills and confidence they need to succeed in these fields.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program is designed to nurture creativity and self-expression in students. Our experienced teachers provide instruction and guidance in a variety of artistic disciplines, including music, visual arts, and drama.

🏈

Athletics

Our Athletics program is designed to promote physical fitness, teamwork, and sportsmanship in students. Our experienced coaches provide guidance and support to help students develop their athletic skills and achieve their goals.
